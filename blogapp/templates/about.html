About UCD GreenLife 🌿

UCD GreenLife is a campus sustainability initiative that helps students and staff take measurable environmental actions.

Our mission is to encourage eco-friendly living through awareness, engagement, and technology.

Project Overview

UCD GreenLife connects the UCD community around sustainable living. The platform turns UCD's sustainability strategy into daily actions through logging, events, and personalized guidance.

Platform User Roles

Students

  • Log sustainable actions (commute, food, waste)
  • Register for events & challenges
  • Track personal carbon footprint trends
  • Earn badges and compete on leaderboards

Staff/Faculty

  • Create and manage sustainability events
  • Publish projects and recruit participants
  • Moderate comments on own events
  • Access advanced analytics

Administrators

  • Full user management capabilities
  • Content moderation tools
  • Campus-wide analytics dashboard
  • Export data for sustainability reports

Our Core Goals

🌱 Behavior Change

Track sustainable actions and reward users with badges and streaks to encourage consistent eco-friendly habits.

👥 Community Building

Foster collaboration through events, discussions, and staff-student sustainability projects.

📊 Data-Driven Insights

Provide anonymized metrics to support campus decision-making and sustainability planning.

🎓 Education & Awareness

Align daily actions with UN Sustainable Development Goals and UCD sustainability initiatives.

Innovative Features

Sustainability Impact Calculator & Visualization Hub

Our unique feature provides real-time, interactive visualization of collective sustainability impact across campus:

Real-time Impact Meter

Live display of campus carbon savings, energy conservation, and waste reduction

Interactive Campus Map

UCD map showing sustainability hotspots and user activities

Growing Forest Visualization

Animated trees grow as carbon savings increase (21kg CO2 = 1 virtual tree)

Department Leaderboards

Friendly competition between faculties with live rankings

Technical Foundation

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), Vue.js, Chart.js, Leaflet.js
  • Backend: Flask with Blueprints, Jinja2 templating
  • Database: SQLite (development) → MySQL (production)
  • Real-time Features: WebSocket for live data updates
  • Security: bcrypt password hashing, CSRF protection, role-based access control

Measuring Our Impact

We track meaningful environmental metrics to demonstrate collective progress:

  • Carbon Savings: Total CO2 reduced through sustainable actions
  • Trees Equivalent: Carbon savings converted to tree planting equivalents
  • Cars Off Road: Equivalent vehicles removed from circulation
  • Energy Conservation: kWh of energy saved through efficient practices
  • Waste Reduction: Kilograms of waste diverted from landfills
