PeakShift analyzes time-of-use electricity pricing to help industrial facilities reduce costs. The system calculates monthly expenses based on your factory's specifications and provides recommendations for shifting production to lower-cost periods.

Peak-Valley Optimization

By analyzing hourly electricity rates and your work schedule, PeakShift identifies opportunities to shift production from peak to valley hours for cost reduction.

Key capabilities:

  • Hourly cost analysis with peak/normal/valley pricing
  • Carbon emission calculation for grid vs. renewable sources
  • Supplier comparison between grid and third-party rates
  • Factory configuration with voltage levels and work schedules
